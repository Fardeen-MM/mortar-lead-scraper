## Is Hiring a College Admissions Consultant Worth It? 

The college admissions process can be immensely stressful for both students and parents. With ever-rising application volumes to the top colleges and universities, it’s never been more challenging to earn acceptances at the most elite schools (e.g. Harvard’s recent [admit rate](https://college.harvard.edu/admissions/admissions-statistics) was 4.59%)! Even the best state universities are becoming increasingly competitive (e.g. UC-Berkeley had record applicants for the fifth straight year). With scholarship dollars on the line, and many previously considered “target schools” now becoming “reach schools,” it’s easy for discussions on the topic to turn emotional. To help navigate this labyrinthine process, many parents turn to the help of a private college admissions counselor. 

### Who can benefit from a college admissions consultant?

Arguably, most students applying to the [top 50 U.S. colleges and universities](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges) (as defined by _U.S. News_) can benefit from the help of a [**college admissions consultant**](#comparison). Most students (and school guidance counselors) fall into the trap of over-emphasizing past accomplishments when completing applications. Instead, students must effectively convey what those achievements suggest about _their future potential_ and how a specific school fits into the picture (along with how their unique “story” will contribute meaningfully to the class). This is easier said than done. 

More specifically, anyone falling into one of the below groupings could likely benefit from college admissions consulting services:

* Applicants to the most elite universities looking to gain an edge over other students
* Students hoping to earn merit-based scholarships at any of the top 50 or so schools
* Those in need of detailed admissions strategy and essay guidance/editing
* International applicants (see **[Admissionado](#admissionado)**) and those in overrepresented demographics applying to competitive programs
* Applicants to highly competitive BS/MD programs (check out [**Quad Education**](https://www.quadeducationgroup.com/end-to-end-application-counseling?utm%5Fsource=examstrategist&utm%5Fmedium=referral&utm%5Fcampaign=partnerships))
* Anyone looking to reduce associated stress, save time and energy, and better streamline the college planning and admissions process

### What to look for when hiring a college admissions coach?

Most college admissions consulting firms offer a free consultation to allow you to better evaluate their services and determine if they are the right fit for you. To aid in this process, our team has put together a list of questions to ask a college admissions counselor before hiring them. 

* What does their consulting model or methodology look like? Will students receive additional checks on their applications aside from just their primary consultant? This is important to ask because different firms use varying methods, and some are better structured and more systematic than others.
* Can they provide case studies or concrete results related to clients’ success rates at top schools? In other words, can they demonstrate that they provide a meaningful edge in the process?
* Do they have administrative backing and prompt turnaround times? Are they able to accommodate priority timelines or other special circumstances?
* What is their professional background and coaching style? Will they be able to challenge your student to look inward and grapple with challenging concepts?

Aside from the primary topics above, be sure to think of questions for any additional needs such as financial aid applications, waitlist support, or interview prep. Some consultants include peripheral services in their packages while others have no capacity to assist in these often-overlooked areas. 

### Self-Evaluate and Define Your Goals

Before deciding whether your student can benefit from a private **[college application consultant](#comparison)**, it’s important to self-reflect and define S.M.A.R.T. goals (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and timely). While an admissions consultant will help guide and refine these, students should ideally have some sense on where they stand and what they hope to accomplish prior to engaging a consultant. 

In order to get the most out of a prospective free admissions consultation, think through the following questions and items beforehand:

* Does your student have specific goals in mind? This is more than simply “gain admission to M.I.T. or Stanford.” Rather, think about specific areas of concern (and opportunities) where an application consultant can help you address or best tell your personal story in a way that’s memorable to admissions committees.
* Are your SAT/ACT scores and GPA at, below, or above the medians of your target schools? Have you defined your safety or reach programs? Don’t feel like you need to have these completely nailed down, as a consultant can really help in the selection process by recommending additional best-fit schools. However, it is good to have placed some thought into your list (and where you stand based on stats alone) before reaching out to firms.
* Assess your level of knowledge about the admissions process and where you may want or need the most help. Some firms offer à la carte services focused on specific components, while others specialize in comprehensive packages and provide a true concierge service.

### Request an Initial Consultation 

After shortlisting a handful of firms, we recommend families schedule an initial consultation (usually done over phone or video meetings). The best college advising firms do this free of charge to help parents and students assess whether they will be an appropriate fit. Remember, this is an opportunity to not only evaluate their own credentials and methodologies but also ask detailed questions on how they can help your student achieve their specific goals in the college admissions arena. 

What we tend to hear from successful past clients is that they knew the relationship would be a good fit from the very start. This results from going into the process with clearly defined goals, outlining your needs, and fully understanding how your prospective consultant can help you achieve them. 

### Services Offered 

When it comes to selecting college admissions services, the biggest distinction to make is whether you’ll want to pay for individual components à la carte or if you’d like a complete end-to-end application support package. Additionally, some firms will charge a flat hourly rate and sell packages composed of a certain number of hours. While this could make sense for a narrow set of applicants, we tend to recommend most parents purchase comprehensive admissions packages to get the best value for your dollar (and achieve a meaningful level of stress reduction). It’s also hard to predict exactly how much time students may need with consultants in advance, which is another reason we tend to prefer all-in packages. For example, our [**top-rated firm**](#admissionado) includes the following components in their complete package:

7. Interview Prep
8. Additional Support

Starting early and taking a proactive approach allows students to build a robust foundation for college applications. Developing a long-term strategic roadmap and _being held accountable to executing_ _it_ by a knowledgeable and trusted advisor is the best insurance policy toward ensuring your student is optimally positioned for college admissions success. Of course, you’ll also avoid a lot of stress and potential conflict by delegating this to an experienced admissions coach and mentor.

The college admissions process is highly involved and demands constant attention to lots of small, but important, details. If you’re a working parent, or navigating multiple children and activities, it’s far too easy for something to slip through the cracks (e.g. your student missed their last opportunity to meet with one of their target school representatives).

## Methodology

Exam Strategist’s **[Best College Admissions Consulting Firms](#reviews)** list is created through a proprietary methodology and includes (though is not limited to) components such as: 

_Depth of Experience_: In addition to years of experience (YOE), we also consider factors such as number of applications reviewed, along with openness of the firm with respect to knowledge-sharing. While most college advising firms work in silos, we give higher marks to those who collaborate openly to serve individual students at the highest level.

_Consulting Model_: Does the firm only pair students with a single consultant? Or do they offer a team model with both a primary consultant and an essay specialist? What about a simulated admissions committee experience? Higher ratings are given to firms that systematically incorporate multiple checks in their process to maximize the value students receive. 

_Administrative Support_: Are the firms’ consultants also running the business, or are they singularly focused on helping clients get into their target programs? 

_Consultant Quality_: How selective is the firm’s interview process? Are the consultants intimately familiar with the admissions processes at the top schools (perhaps having earned a degree from one themselves)? More importantly, do they have the raw teaching and coaching talent to help your student achieve their full admissions potential?

_Demonstrated Results_: Does the consultant share concrete results or case studies of past clients? Do they have a track record of helping students gain acceptances to the most competitive U.S. colleges and universities? 

_Client Feedback_: Are there any noticeably concerning and consistent themes that stand out? How enthusiastic are past clients in recommending the firm to their friends and colleagues? Did the advisor add value to the student’s application in specific and meaningful ways? 

_Global Presence_: Given that college admissions is a global playing field, does the firm efficiently and effectively serve students from around the world? Do they have the requisite background to appropriately assist international applicants? 

We believe these are the crucial factors to consider when deciding who to hire for college admissions guidance. While other sites may feature bizarre (and in our opinion, bogus) overly-quantitative scoring components that make better sense for comparing running shoes than high-end consulting services, we focus on what matters to prospective clients. In other words: how to choose a firm that will highlight _not what your student is today, but what they will become in the future_.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I begin working with a college application consultant? 

Although the typical student doesn’t start thinking about college counseling services until the spring of their junior year, in our opinion, the earlier students and families start preparing for college applications the better. 

That being said, part of a good college application consultant’s job is to get students on the right track to meet necessary deadlines while putting the best application forward. Additionally, most students are busy with extracurriculars, achieving great grades, and scoring high on standardized exams before thinking about their applications. So, most families will tend to decide on a college admissions consultant in the late spring or early summer just after their students finished junior year. 

### How much do college admissions consultants cost? 

The costs to hire a college admissions consultant varies considerably across firms and are based on the services needed. At the very low end, essay editing packages start around $200, while the most expensive comprehensive admissions consulting package we’ve seen listed costs a mind-blowing $85,000\. However, the average starting cost for a complete admissions package from our **[top-ranked firms](#comparison)** is around $5,000-$7,000\. 

### Are there any affordable college admissions consulting services? 

While many of the well-known, private college advising firms may cost an arm and a leg, we’ve highlighted several firms on our list with more affordable admissions consulting services. For example, **[Admit Advantage](#admitadvantage)**, our best-value firm, offers comprehensive guidance for under $4,000.

### Is virtual admissions counseling an effective way to coach students? 

Yes, in our opinion, virtual admissions counseling is not only an effective way to coach students, but it’s also quickly becoming the norm. While some students may have the option (albeit an expensive one) to visit some of the top firms in person, most families can’t afford the big name admissions consultants. Or they live outside of the major metropolitan areas where there aren’t many valuable options available. Virtual admissions consulting provides more affordable access to some of the leading consultants from anywhere in the world. 

### Does working with a college admissions advisor guarantee admission to my target schools? 

Absolutely not. Although working with a top private college admissions advisor has been shown to significantly increase your child’s odds of admission to their target schools, it in no way guarantees admission. 

### Should my student work with an admissions consultant if they don’t have an outstanding SAT or GPA? 

If your student doesn’t have an outstanding SAT or GPA, there can still be instances where working with an admissions consultant can provide tremendous value. Ultimately, the definition of a great SAT or GPA will depend on the school itself. Admissions consultants can help families with school selection and other fit criteria. If your child’s SAT or GPA is just below the median of their target schools, working with an admissions consultant can be well worth the cost. 

### Are college admissions consultants worth it? 

To determine if working with a college admissions consultant is right for you, first consider your goals and critically evaluate where you may stand relative to the class profiles of your target schools. For students who wish to attend the top 50 colleges and universities, it usually makes sense to work with a college admissions consultant to increase your chances of acceptance. To learn more, read our **[complete discussion](#worth)** on the topic.

### How do I choose a college admissions consultant? 

Families should choose a college admissions consultant not only based on the firm’s past admissions experience, but also on their raw coaching and teaching abilities. Additionally, you’ll want to consider their consulting model (i.e. one person does it all vs. the support of a full team) and what areas of the application process they specialize in.
